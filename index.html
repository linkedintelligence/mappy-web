Mappy turns messy human intent into structured place-search signals, finds candidates, ranks them, and tells you why they fit.

Understands intent

“4-star hotel with Wi-Fi” becomes actual constraints instead of a keyword pile.

Finds candidates

Searches OpenStreetMap data across multiple place types instead of locking onto one category.

Ranks intelligently

Distance, category fit, amenities and other signals work together to produce useful results.

“I don't know which filters I need. I just know what I want.”

That's the point.

Mappy is built around the way people actually describe places. No filter archaeology required.

Want a quiet place to work? A 4-star hotel? A cheap date-night spot? Just say it.

Built to be open.

The creators of Mappy believe the future of AI is open. The core of Mappy is on GitHub, hackable, inspectable, and forkable.
